# Product imagery

The 18 catalog images were regenerated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on solid white (#FFFFFF) backgrounds. Final images are 1200 × 900 JPG files in `public/assets/products/`, using the existing product filenames. Original advertisements remain in `public/assets/products/ads/`.

The final prompt sets and generation references are recorded in:

- [Full-colour camera packages](image-prompts-full-color.json)
- [IR camera packages, Dahua package, networking and hard drive](image-prompts-ir-network.json)
- [Door locks and intercoms](image-prompts-access.json)

The hero section and its original imagery are retained.
